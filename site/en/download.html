FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is this allowed by Riot?

Yes. MVP Tracker doesn't interact with the game and doesn't read any memory: it only uses public match data, exactly like existing trackers. No risk of a ban.

Do I need to give my Riot password?

Never. The app only asks for your Valorant name and tag (your public Riot ID). Your MVP Tracker account is completely separate from your Riot account.

Is it paid?

MVP Tracker is free.

How many matches are analyzed?

Each sync fetches your last 50 matches, and the app keeps them in a local cache. Your history grows over time, beyond what the API exposes at any single moment.

Can I look up another player's profile?

Yes, the search bar at the top lets you look up any public Riot ID. Your personal data (wrapped, goals, tilt, network, collection) always stays tied to your own account, even while browsing someone else's.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

Not right now. The app is distributed for Windows only. A version for other systems isn't ruled out later on, but it isn't the priority.
